📚 Gramática Intermedia

Present Perfect vs Past Simple

Present Perfect: I have lived here for 5 years.
(Uso: experiencias, acciones que continúan)
Past Simple: I lived in Paris in 2020.
(Uso: acciones terminadas en tiempo específico)

Conditional Sentences (Tipo 1 y 2)

First Conditional: If it rains, I will stay home.
Second Conditional: If I were rich, I would travel the world.

📰 Comprensión de Lectura

Texto de Práctica

"The Benefits of Learning Languages"

Learning a second language has numerous advantages. Moreover, research shows that bilingual people have better problem-solving skills. Although it can be challenging at first, the long-term benefits are significant. Therefore, many educators recommend starting language learning at an early age.

Preguntas de Comprensión

1. What does research show about bilingual people?
2. Why do educators recommend early language learning?
3. Find three connector words in the text.

💬 Práctica de Conversación

Expresiones Útiles

Dar opiniones:
• "In my opinion..."
• "I believe that..."
• "From my point of view..."
Mostrar acuerdo/desacuerdo:
• "I completely agree with you."
• "I'm afraid I disagree."
• "That's a good point, but..."

Situación de Práctica

Tema: Discussing travel experiences

Pregunta: "What's the most interesting place you have ever visited? Why did you find it interesting?"

Practica usando Present Perfect y expresiones de opinión.

✍️ Escritura Intermedia

Estructura de Párrafos

1. Topic Sentence (Oración principal)
2. Supporting Details (Detalles de apoyo)
3. Concluding Sentence (Oración de conclusión)

Tarea de Escritura

Tema: Write a paragraph about your favorite hobby

Usa al menos 3 conectores y 100-120 palabras.

Conectores sugeridos: First, Additionally, However, Finally

🎧 Comprensión Auditiva

Estrategias de Escucha

Pre-listening: Predice el contenido
While-listening: Toma notas de palabras clave
Post-listening: Verifica tu comprensión

Tipos de Audio para Practicar

• Noticias cortas (BBC Learning English)
• Conversaciones telefónicas
• Presentaciones breves
• Podcasts para estudiantes
